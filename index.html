A living pond

Every ring is simulated, not animated. The surface is solved on the GPU as a volume-conserving shallow-water field, with a second dispersive ripple layer for the small waves a pond actually makes.

Throwhold to charge, release to throw. aim with the cursor.
Touchdrag through the water. push it, swirl it, lift your hand.
Feedscatter food. the koi will come, and the ripples are real.
Orbitright-drag (or two fingers) to orbit. scroll to zoom.

Floating objects displace real volume and read the simulated surface back from the GPU. Fish flee the same disturbance events that move the water. Two-way coupling, all the way down.
